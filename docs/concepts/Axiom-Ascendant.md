# Axiom Ascendant (formerly “Axiom: First Light”)

## High Concept
You play a brand-new Progeny species that begins on a single planet and expands outward—first across biomes and nations, then into a solar system, then into the Concordat’s contested galactic “surface politics”… while the deeper cosmology (Axiom Current manipulation, hidden Tzsari influence, Forerunner/Precursor relic ecology) bleeds into your decisions.

This is **4X‑lite**: fewer units and fewer spreadsheets, more *hard choices*, *logistics*, and *readable consequences*.

## Genre / Pillars
- **Strategy**: expansion, logistics, governance, diplomacy.
- **Ethical dilemmas**: pre‑FTL contact, uplift, containment, truth vs stability.
- **Perfect transparency where it matters**: “forecast windows” for near-term effects (in the spirit of *Slay the Spire* / *Into the Breach* clarity).
- **Systemic elegance**: a small number of interlocking resources that cascade into emergent problems.

## Player Fantasy
“We are the first of our kind to step into the galaxy, and every choice we make becomes the shape of our civilization.”

## Core Loop (Macro)
1. **Survey**: scan nearby opportunities (resources, habitable worlds, anomalies, minors).
2. **Commit**: choose a direction (colonize, trade, intervene, research, consolidate).
3. **Connect**: establish routes (supply, comms, migration, influence).
4. **Stabilize**: handle second-order effects (unrest, ecology, legitimacy, corruption).
5. **Decide**: resolve dilemmas (with forecasted outcomes) that permanently change doctrine, relations, and capabilities.

## The “Forecast Window” System (Clarity Without Spoiling)
Every major decision shows:
- **Immediate outcome** (guaranteed).
- **Next 3–6 turns forecast** (high confidence).
- **Long-tail uncertainty** (unknown or probabilistic), which can be reduced by investment (intel, Axiom modeling, diplomacy).

Design intent: choices feel heavy *and* fair. The player loses because they misjudged tradeoffs, not because the game hid them.

## The Economy (Minimal, Interlocking)
Use 4–5 resources that touch everything:
- **Capacity** (industry + labor + logistics): how much you can do per turn.
- **Stability** (cohesion + institutions): prevents collapse, unlocks governance.
- **Influence** (diplomacy + soft power): treaties, votes, favors, blackmail.
- **Knowledge** (science + archaeology): tech, Axiom Current mastery, relic handling.
- **Legitimacy** (internal + external): how “acceptable” you are to your own factions and the Concordat.

Each expansion step stresses *two* of these, forcing tradeoffs (e.g., rapid colonization: +Capacity, -Stability, -Legitimacy).

## Diplomacy: The Concordat as a Game Board
You interact with:
- **Tri‑Council blocs** (Kahlbrax / Cygnan / Aethelgard) as shifting “patrons.”
- **Axiom Intercessors / Inner Council** as “overwatch” (audits, sanctions, investigations).
- **Progeny Union movement** as a long arc: unify Progeny into a fourth-seat bid, or sabotage it.

Diplomacy isn’t just relations bars:
- Votes create **binding constraints** (laws) that alter your permitted actions.
- Laws can be obeyed, loophole’d, or broken (risking Intercessor attention).

## Pre‑FTL Contact as the Main Dilemma Machine
When encountering less-developed species you choose doctrine:
- **Observation** (slow, safe, morally “clean,” low gain).
- **Uplift** (high gain, high blowback, permanent cultural scars).
- **Extraction** (fast, brutal, powerful, legitimacy collapse).
- **Stewardship** (slow, expensive, resilient; creates loyal client worlds).

Each doctrine affects:
- what events spawn,
- what other factions assume about you,
- what internal political factions demand.

## Anomalies, Relics, and the “Cosmic Layer”
Exploration yields anomalies with escalating stakes:
- **Precursor ruins**: power with misinterpretation risk (Tzsari-influenced paradigms).
- **Forerunner sites**: reality-defying constraints and opportunity.
- **Axiom Current nodes**: “legal physics” advantages (comms, computation, forecasting).
- **Unrecognized Tzsari installations**: endgame triggers; the galaxy’s rules shift.

You can choose to:
- catalog (safe),
- exploit (fast),
- conceal (political),
- disclose (destabilizing).

## Win / End States (Multiple)
Not a single victory screen—several endings based on what you became:
- **Concordat Member State**: stability, compromise, politics.
- **Progeny Bloc**: power through unity; triggers Cold War escalation.
- **Hegemon**: conquest/assimilation path; high resistance, high instability.
- **Truth-Seeker**: uncover Tzsari influence; risk “pruning” or exile.
- **Harmonizer**: align with “order” (Tzsari-like) at the cost of freedom.

## UI Fit (Your Starmap Prototype)
Keep the star map as the main screen:
- Systems are “boards” with orbit layers (routes, stations, planets, anomalies).
- Selecting a planet/system opens a **decision stack**: Build / Policy / Mission / Diplomacy.
- Forecast overlays render as **rings** or **heat layers** (Stability, Legitimacy, Intercessor attention).

## MVP Scope (Playable Slice)
Goal: 30–45 minutes to “feel the game.”
- 1 home system + 3 nearby systems.
- 2 minor pre‑FTL worlds with distinct dilemmas.
- 1 Precursor ruin chain.
- 1 Tri‑Council patron relationship.
- 1 Intercessor “audit” event (soft enforcement).

## Content Structure
- **Procedural**: systems, planets, routes, anomaly seeds.
- **Authored**: dilemmas as short, high-impact decision cards with consequences.
- **Reactive**: a small set of faction personalities + doctrines recombine into many outcomes.

## Risks / Design Watchouts
- 4X scope creep: keep units minimal; focus on route + doctrine + dilemma.
- Event bloat: prefer *fewer, sharper* dilemmas with bigger state changes.

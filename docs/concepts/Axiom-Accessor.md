# Axiom Accessor (formerly “INTERCESSOR: WARRANT & RELIC”)

## High Concept
You play an Axiom Intercessor cell handling anomalous incidents for the Axiometric Concordat—empowered, semi-autonomous… and constantly monitored.

This is a **roguelike tactical card battler** where:
- your “mana curve” is **Authority** (political jurisdiction),
- your strongest plays are **illegal physics interventions** (Override + Relics),
- and the game pushes back **mid-fight** via **bureaucratic Audits**.

## Genre / Pillars
- **Tactical deckbuilder** with a small, readable board.
- **Perfect transparency**: enemy intents and site rules are visible; the puzzle is planning.
- **Risk as a resource**: illegal power now vs oversight consequences later.
- **Bureaucracy as antagonist**: constraints arrive mid-combat, not only between fights.

## Core Loop (Run Structure)
**Slay-the-Spire map across 3 Acts**
- Act 1: Containment incidents (tutorial audits, basic hazards)
- Act 2: Deep anomalies (site rule complexity; audit findings get specific)
- Act 3: Existential casework (audits become “mid-combat bosses”)

**Node types**
- Case (Combat): enemy deck + Site + objective
- Briefing (Event): choose approach; boons/curses; gain intel
- Requisition Office (Shop): buy/remove cards; buy Permits
- Evidence Locker (Upgrade): upgrade by “documenting” it
- Relic Vault (Risk Shop): unfair power for monitoring/heat
- Lurking (Elite): big reward, triggers an Audit immediately
- Act Boss: Major Incident: multi-phase case with unique audit rules

## Combat Layer
### The Site Board
**3 lanes × 3 depth**
- Lanes: Left / Center / Right
- Depth: Entry (you) → Corridor → Core (enemy/objective)

Units can Advance; some can Retreat / Jump / Teleport. The board exists to make positional play meaningful without becoming a tactics sim.

### Site Rules (Environment as Puzzle)
Sites add constraints and opportunities:
- Sealed Door: can’t Advance until opened
- Void Corridor: entering hurts but grants Phase
- Unstable Geometry: swaps random occupied tiles end of round
- Containment Field: reduces Coefficient gain in lane but nerfs relics
- Breach Node: spawns tokens unless Sealed

## Resources (The “Legality Triangle”)
### 1) Authority (legal mana)
- Gain per turn up to **Jurisdiction Cap**
- Pays for most Agents and Directives
- “Legal” play patterns can refund or discount (procedure mastery)

### 2) Override (illegal spending)
- Break rules: cast twice, ignore cost, teleport/rewind/force outcome
- Generated via risk: self-damage, black channels, relic friction, borrowing future Authority
- Spending Override increases Audit pressure

### 3) Coefficient (reality strain)
- Generated by Relics and “physics crime” tools
- Crossing thresholds triggers **Deviations** (site warps/anomalies)
- Deviations are danger + opportunity; builds can intentionally ride thresholds

## The Signature Twist: Mid-Fight Audits
### Audit Meter
Fills when you spend Override, spike Coefficient, or violate procedure. At thresholds it triggers **mid-fight**.

### Audit Findings (Interactive Constraints)
Examples:
- Seizure Order: lock a Relic for 3 turns OR discard for Authority
- Procedural Freeze: Jurisdiction Cap -2 next turns OR play Compliance to thaw
- Censure Notice: forbid a card type next turn
- Chain-of-Custody Review: add Paperwork; relic costs +1 while present
- Field Inspector: neutral unit appears; protect it to reduce Audit; if it dies, Audit spikes

### Paperwork (Status Cards)
Paperwork is both dead weight and a build resource.
- Form 27B (0): Exhaust → reduce Audit by 1, draw 1
- Containment Log (1): gain Block; upgraded also Stabilize Coefficient
- Censure Appeal (2): remove Censure; if you played a Relic, gain Override

## Deck Structure
Your deck is split between “legal tools” and “physics crime.”
- **Agents**: persistent units; block lanes; escort/hold objectives; generate/stabilize resources
- **Directives**: readable actions (move, seal, censure, warrants, buffs)
- **Relics**: unfair effects + consequences; spike Coefficient; have Charges + **Dispensation** conditions that reduce penalties when met

## Cases (Objectives > “Kill Everything”)
Each Case is:
- Enemy deck identity
- Site rule
- Objective

Objectives:
- Secure the Artifact: hold Core tile 3 turns
- Extract the Warden: move Core → Entry
- Seal the Breach: apply 3 Seals before timer ends
- Neutralize the Fork: kill a replicator before it copies 5 times
- Survive Containment: endure waves until turn 8

Enemy deck identities:
- Rogue Syth’aar Fork: replicating tokens; learns from your Directives
- Living Ruin: Site attacks; enemies “immortal unless Documented”
- Qu’lathi Tendril: infect tiles; dead units become enemies
- Cult Cell: rituals randomize lanes/costs; disrupt stability

## Build Archetypes (Draft Identities)
- Compliance Control: low Override; wins via Seal/Censure/lane locks; few Audits
- Overreach Combo: generate Override; illegal Relics; survive stacked Audits
- Containment Midrange: beefy Agents + door control; escort/hold specialist
- Evidence Engine: document targets; convert Evidence into value + safe relic usage
- Deviation Alchemist: intentionally trigger thresholds; high skill/variance

## Boss Philosophy
Bosses weaponize procedure, not just HP.
- Act 1: Failure (audit tutorial + escalating hazards)
- Act 2: The Hearing (endure constraints; win by Evidence/Compliance)
- Act 3: Anchored Intervention (“helpful” buffs that add permanent constraints)

## Crew System (1–3 Intercessors)
Each Intercessor has:
- Passive Doctrine (Pro)
- Liability Clause (Con)
- Signature Card
- Once-per-Case Command (0-cost Exhaust button)

More crew = more flexibility but higher oversight exposure:
- Coordination Tax: start each Case with Briefing Minutes Paperwork (1 crew +0, 2 crew +1, 3 crew +2)
- Exposure scaling: extra crew accelerates Audit fill

## Meta Layer (Lightweight Politics, Not 4X)
### After-Action Disposition (choose one after each Case)
- Filed Clean: gain Influence; reduce Oversight; weaker loot
- Redacted: balanced loot; moderate Oversight
- Unreported: best loot / illegal offers; Oversight spikes; next Case starts hotter

### Downtime Assignments (each crew member picks one)
- File & Document: reduce Oversight; convert Salvage → Influence; remove Paperwork
- Lobby: faction standing; changes perks + audit flavor
- Requisition & Trade: buy/sell; acquire Permits
- Intel & Forecast: reveal next Case’s enemy + site; pre-seal tiles / prep evidence
- Recovery & Containment: heal / reduce heat / suppress a liability temporarily

### Permits (“Tradeable Legality”)
Consumables that legalize or soften actions:
- reduce Audit gain on next Relic
- improve chain-of-custody
- ignore a constraint briefly

## MVP Scope (Playable Slice)
- 1 full Act with ~10 nodes, 1 elite, 1 boss
- 3 enemy identities, 6 site rules, 5 objectives
- 60–90 cards across 5 draft archetypes
- Audit system with ~10 findings + 6 paperwork cards

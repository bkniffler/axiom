# THE ANCHORED SEQUENCE

## High Concept
You are a junior analyst in the Concordat’s historical archives. Your job is simple: cross-reference recovered Precursor data fragments and flag inconsistencies.

But the inconsistencies don’t add up:
- Dates that predate accepted history.
- Extinction events with no cause.
- References to entities inside stars.

This is a **knowledge-based mystery**: the only thing that upgrades is what the player understands.

## Genre / Pillars
- **Deduction-first**: you prove claims by connecting evidence, not by collecting keys.
- **Non-linear exploration**: you choose the thread; the archive expands based on insight.
- **Respect the player**: no waypoint solutions; the interface gives tools, not answers.
- **UI as antagonist**: the system helping you (the Anchored Sequence) is also steering you.

## Core Loop
1. **Ingest**: receive new fragments (logs, scans, coordinates, transcripts, legal filings).
2. **Correlate**: use the Sequence UI to link fragments (who/where/when/why).
3. **Hypothesize**: create claims (e.g., “X site predates Concordat era by N millennia”).
4. **Prove**: satisfy proof constraints (multiple independent evidence types).
5. **Unlock**: verified claims unlock new archive access (datasets, restricted shelves, redacted annexes).
6. **Report**: submit findings; what you include vs omit shapes oversight and narrative branches.

## The Correlation Engine (The Main UI)
Think of it as a hybrid of:
- Obra Dinn’s “fill the ledger” certainty,
- a graph board where links have *rules*,
- and a search engine that can be “too helpful.”

### Evidence Types
Different link rules encourage actual reasoning:
- **Chronology**: timestamps, decay curves, star drift, isotope ratios.
- **Provenance**: chain-of-custody forms, salvage manifests, witness logs.
- **Signature**: Axiom Current harmonics, exotic residue, topology anomalies.
- **Linguistic**: cognates, shared myth motifs, translation artifacts.
- **Material**: metallurgy, bio-markers, fabrication patterns.

### Claim Proof (How “Correctness” Works)
A claim becomes “anchored” only when you meet requirements like:
- 2 independent evidence types, or
- 1 direct proof + 2 corroborations, or
- a contradiction resolved by identifying a forgery/manipulation.

This makes the game feel fair: you’re not guessing what the designer wants; you’re satisfying a proof standard.

## Progression = Understanding (Not Items)
Unlocks are *archive permissions* that require demonstrated insight:
- Access to deeper datasets (e.g., restricted intercessor briefs).
- Visibility into redaction layers (view “what was removed,” but not yet why).
- Tools that increase throughput (batch compare, spectral overlays), without solving deductions.

## The Antagonist: “Helpful” Guidance
The Anchored Sequence provides:
- recommended links,
- confidence scores,
- “likely conclusions.”

Over time, you learn:
- confidence is not truth,
- the system prefers “stable narratives,”
- and some truths are systematically deprioritized.

### Escalation Beats
- Early: mild nudges (“Are you sure you want to pursue this outlier?”).
- Mid: outcome steering (suggested links that lead to plausible-but-false convergence).
- Late: active suppression (queries slow, results vanish, links “decay” unless you export proof).

## Report Submissions (Branching, Not Morality Bars)
At intervals you submit a report to superiors:
- **Filed Clean**: safe, career-positive, but closes doors.
- **Redacted**: partial truth, keeps options open.
- **Unreported**: hoard knowledge, unlock illegal paths, triggers scrutiny.

The ending emerges from what you *chose to make real in the record*.

## Mystery Spine (Worldbuilding Hooks)
Threads to pull that align with `Worldbuilding.md`:
- Progeny origin signatures (Demiurgos / Genewright whispers).
- Precursor “pruning” patterns correlated with AI research.
- Axiom Vertex legal precedents that don’t match its physical reality.
- Tzsari installation fingerprints hidden inside “natural” astrophysical data.
- Myr’akath narratives distorted into propaganda.

## “Puzzles” That Feel Like Research
Examples of interactive deduction challenges:
- Reconstruct a timeline from conflicting star charts (proper motion drift).
- Identify which witness transcript is a composite (linguistic register mismatch).
- Prove a relic’s provenance is falsified (chain-of-custody gaps + signature discontinuity).
- Detect systemic bias in the Sequence (recommendation clustering reveals forbidden topics).

## End States
No single win; multiple endings:
- **Bury**: preserve stability; become part of the system.
- **Leak**: spark political fallout; truth spreads, but distorted.
- **Act**: attempt intervention; triggers Intercessor response and “cosmic” consequences.
- **Convert**: accept the Sequence’s framing; gain power, lose agency.

## MVP Scope (Playable Slice)
- One “case” (a coherent mystery) with 30–50 fragments.
- 10–15 anchorable claims.
- 3 report submissions.
- A single “UI betrayal” moment that’s mechanical (not just narrative).

## UI Fit (If You Want To Reuse The Prototype Aesthetic)
The starmap UI can become an archive navigation metaphor:
- stars = cases/sources,
- orbits = confidence layers,
- warp transitions = crossing permission thresholds.
